# Data Quality and Provenance

This document describes the bibliographical verification, scope, limitations and source provenance of the *University Mathematical Texts* bibliographical inventory.

The initial bibliographical survey was conducted by 8 November 2026. The current documentation reflects the dataset as of 24 November 2026.

## Bibliographical verification

The inventory combines information from institutional catalogues, contemporary publisher advertisements, publication histories and identifying pages of digitized books. The records distinguish between different levels of bibliographical verification.

### Publication records

| Verification status              | Records |
|----------------------------------|--------:|
| Catalogue-supported, provisional |     141 |
| Identifying pages inspected      |      12 |
| Metadata lead only               |      27 |
| **Total**                        | **180** |

Inspection of identifying pages establishes information such as the title, date, edition designation or publication history of a digital witness. It does not imply inspection of the entire volume or collation against other editions.

The publication records represent provisional groupings of editions, impressions, issues and selected later reissues. They do not necessarily correspond to distinct revisions of mathematical content.

Access conditions reflect the time of the survey and may change. Public accessibility does not establish public-domain status or permission to redistribute a digital copy.

## Scope and limitations

- **Series coverage.** A final publisher list has not yet been located. The identified roster may therefore be incomplete. Some first-publication dates and eight series numbers remain to be verified.
- **Publication histories.** Edition, impression and issue groupings may require further differentiation or consolidation as additional copies are examined.
- **Holdings.** Institutional holdings counts are based on retrieved catalogue records rather than comprehensive collection surveys. Princeton-owned copies are distinguished from ReCAP partner holdings. The records do not establish current availability or reader eligibility.
- **Digital objects and physical copies.** The published tables summarize the holdings and digital-access survey. They do not include a complete copy-level inventory or digital-object manifest, and therefore do not permit independent reconstruction of all aggregate holdings counts or deduplication decisions.
- **Contributors and translations.** Coverage remains partial, particularly concerning biographical information, editorial responsibilities and translated editions.
- **Missing information.** Blank fields indicate information that has not been recorded, has not yet been established or is not applicable to the particular record.

The tables have been checked for structural consistency, identifier uniqueness and referential integrity. These checks do not constitute independent bibliographical verification of every record.

Field definitions and verification categories are documented in the [data dictionary](DATA_DICTIONARY.md).

## Selected bibliographical notes

The following cases illustrate uncertainties and distinctions relevant to reconstructing the publication history of the series.

- **A. C. Aitken, *Statistical Mathematics*.** The third edition of 1944 (`E-AIT-SM-1944-3`) is supported by a [digitized copy](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.212078) whose title and publication-history pages were inspected. The 1942 record (`E-AIT-SM-1942-2`) cites a later copy as potential publication-history evidence rather than a matching digital witness. The fourth revised edition is dated 1945 in an inspected publication history, whereas an OCR derivative gives 1946.
- **W. H. McCrea, *Analytical Geometry of Three Dimensions*.** The publication history of the 1947 edition identifies the 1945 publication as a reprint of the 1942 first edition.
- **E. G. Phillips, *Functions of a Complex Variable*.** An inspected 1961 copy is an impression of the eighth edition of 1957, while a separate catalogue record describes a ninth edition dated 1961. The relationship between these records remains to be established.
- **G. C. Shephard, *Vector Spaces of Finite Dimension*.** An Open Library record dated 1965 remains a provisional bibliographical lead alongside the currently identified first-publication date of 1966.

## Sources and provenance

The inventory was assembled through the comparison, selection and organization of bibliographical information from multiple sources.

Source URLs are retained in the relevant tables. They identify principal references for individual records rather than an exhaustive list of bibliographical witnesses. In some cases, later editions provide evidence of earlier publications through their publication histories.
